## Still to supply, only when the Foundation has them

- A founder portrait and the founder story, if the Foundation wants either on the About page. The story section stays hidden while the text is empty.
- Replacement trustee portraits if any of the imported files are the wrong person or the wrong crop. Upload them on the trustee screen with the person’s name as the alt text.
- Affiliate wordmarks once the secretariat names each organisation. Until then the five codes above are the only labels.
- News and event photographs only for posts the Foundation writes. Do not stage stock “students in London” or similar theme imagery.
- Scholar portraits only after that awardee ticks showcase consent. The scholars page shows initials when there is no photo.
- Passport photographs belong to applications. They stay outside the web root and are never reused on the public site.

## When a new photograph is taken

- Ask for written consent that names the page where the picture will appear.
- Shoot in daylight, with the person’s face unobstructed for a trustee portrait, and keep the original file.
- Export a JPEG or PNG under 1 MB for the web. The server can also make a WebP copy.
- Write alt text that names the person or the place. Do not use “image” or a stock caption.
- Do not photograph an applicant’s documents, NIN slip, or ID card for the public site.
